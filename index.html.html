🔌 Live calculations

RMS voltage
Vrms = Vpeak ÷ √2
RMS current (Ohm’s law)
Irms = Vrms ÷ R
Average resistor power loss
P = Irms²R
1

Current reverses

The source swaps + and − every half cycle, so the current arrow and dots keep changing direction.

2

RMS gives the heating

RMS is the steady DC value that would heat the resistor equally. It is the peak divided by √2.

3

Frequency does not change heat

For a plain resistor, a faster swing changes how quickly the current reverses, not the average power.
